Your 3 options
1 · Just the paint (do it yourself)—
Buy the paint & the bits, and do the work yourself. See exactly what to buy at Builders below.
2 · Buy paint + hire a painter—
You buy the paint, and pay an individual painter for the labour only (about R20–25 per m²). You manage the job.
3 · Full contractor—
A contractor buys all the paint & materials, brings transport, ladders, tools and safety equipment, and does the whole job. The priciest option, but by far the least hassle — nothing for you to arrange.
Options 1 & 2 assume typical mid-range paint. Pick a cheaper or premium paint in "What to buy" below to see the exact cost.
